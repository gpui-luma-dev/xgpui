Why color math

Stock GPUI colours are sRGB (Hsla, Rgba), and its gradients blend two stops in sRGB or Oklab. That is fine until colour comes from somewhere else: a photo tagged Adobe RGB or ProPhoto, a design in Display P3, a wide-gamut display, an ICC profile. GPUI has no way to convert between those spaces, and nothing to decide what happens to a colour the target cannot show. Clamping the channels yourself shifts the hue.

xgpui-color is that colour math, standalone: no GPUI, window or GPU needed. It converts between sRGB, Display P3, Rec.2020, Adobe RGB (1998) and ProPhoto RGB through XYZ, OkLab and OkLCh; maps out-of-gamut colours explicitly (clip, or reduce chroma at fixed lightness and hue); samples gradients in linear RGB, OkLab or OkLCh; reads custom RGB metadata and, optionally, ICC profiles; and ships the same math as WGSL, so CPU and GPU agree.

Example: a photo colour sRGB can't show

Converting reveals that the colour is out of range, and mapping brings it in without changing its hue:

use xgpui_color::{EncodedRgb, GamutMapping, RgbSpace};

// A saturated cyan from an Adobe RGB photo, shown on an sRGB display.
let cyan = EncodedRgb::new(RgbSpace::AdobeRgb1998, [0., 1., 1.])?.decode()?;

let raw = cyan.convert(RgbSpace::Srgb)?;
assert!(raw.components()[0] < 0.); // out of gamut: sRGB can't show it as is

// Map it in: keep lightness and hue, give up just enough chroma.
let shown = cyan.map_gamut(RgbSpace::Srgb, GamutMapping::ReduceChroma)?;
let [r, g, b] = shown.encode()?.components(); // encoded sRGB, ready for gpui::Rgba

Turning on P3 is the easy part

A wide-gamut window (xgpui-gamut) lets the display show more colour. Showing the right colour is the science, and it is where most wide-gamut bugs come from:

  • Every colour needs its space. (1, 0, 0) is a different red in sRGB and in P3. Pass existing sRGB values to a P3 surface unconverted and the whole UI comes out oversaturated.
  • HSL is not a colour space. It is RGB in a different shape, so HSL in sRGB and HSL in P3 are different colours, and its lightness is not what the eye sees. Use OkLCh for pickers and palettes.
  • Maths belongs in linear light. Blending, gradients and alpha on encoded values give muddy midpoints and dark fringes.
  • Some colours do not fit. Adobe RGB, ProPhoto and Rec.2020 reach outside P3; clamping channels shifts the hue, so choose a mapping.
  • CPU and GPU must agree, or a swatch will not match the fill it describes.

If everything is sRGB on sRGB output, plain sRGB and HSL are enough. The science starts the moment a second colour space appears.

See color math for gradients and the WGSL helpers, and color tasks for end-to-end recipes.

Public surface at a glance

EncodedRgb, LinearRgb, RgbSpace, XyzD65, Oklab, Oklch, GamutMapping, LinearRgba, LinearGradient and WGSL_COLOR_MATH. No GPUI or GPU context required. Browser color demo reports its sRGB canvas separately from P3 display capability; a native companion displays paired sRGB/P3 samples.
