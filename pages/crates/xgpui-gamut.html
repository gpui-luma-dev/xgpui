Public surface at a glance

WideColor::linear_p3 supports CustomContent; optional instanced WidePainter/WideGradient use explicit P3 shaders. Shared color math lives in xgpui-color; GPUI backends own presentation. Native color-lab compares P3 and mapped sRGB reference swatches with effective window/display status.
