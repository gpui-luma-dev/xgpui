# GPUI 1.28.1 upstream change accounting

For [issue #284](https://github.com/gpui-luma-dev/xgpui/issues/284), epic completion
criterion 1: every upstream change in scope is accounted for as adopted, adapted,
already covered, or deferred with a reason. The source list is the 78 files that
upstream changed between the pinned 1.27.0 and 1.28.1 snapshots in the
[comparison](1.28.1-comparison.json). Paths are relative to `gpui/crates/`.

Conflict policy throughout: XGPUI's code wins. "Adapted" means upstream's change was
ported into XGPUI's version and keeps XGPUI's behavior on conflict. "Not adopted" means
the upstream change would have replaced deliberate XGPUI behavior.

Status as of November 14, 2026: packages 01–11 and 13 are merged into `issue-284/epic`; 12 is this final review.
